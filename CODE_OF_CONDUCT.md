# Code of Conduct

Mekasa contributors must create a respectful, accessible and evidence-focused community.

We welcome people regardless of disability, access method, age, body, race, ethnicity, nationality, language, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, religion, experience, education or socioeconomic status. Ableism, harassment, intimidation, unwanted disclosure of personal or health information, and pressure to participate in research are unacceptable.

When discussing access needs, center the person's stated preferences. Do not present a diagnosis as a complete description of a person, and do not treat technical convenience as more important than autonomy, privacy or communication.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments or participation that violate these expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately to a maintainer through the hosting platform until a dedicated confidential address is published. Avoid sharing more personal information than the report requires.
