# Governance

Mekasa currently uses maintainer stewardship while the contributor community forms. Technical changes are reviewed for correctness, privacy, accessibility, reproducibility and contract stability. Decisions that affect vocabulary, interaction burden, calibration experience or human research require meaningful AAC-user and relevant interdisciplinary participation.

No maintainer may describe the project as clinically validated without corresponding reviewed evidence. Human-subject research is governed by applicable institutional, ethical and legal processes rather than by a repository vote.

Architecture decisions are recorded in `docs/adr`. Significant reversals add a new ADR that supersedes the old one rather than silently rewriting history.
