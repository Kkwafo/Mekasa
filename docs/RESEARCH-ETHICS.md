# Research ethics

Mekasa does not automatically initiate research with people. Developer self-testing and synthetic tests do not establish suitability for AAC users.

Any future study involving people must determine and follow applicable institutional, ethical and legal review. At minimum, planning must address:

- accessible informed consent/assent and communication support;
- the right to decline, pause and withdraw without penalty;
- avoidance of coercion, especially where participants depend on caregivers or services;
- a working fallback communication method throughout participation;
- purpose limitation, data minimization, access control, retention and deletion;
- separate opt-in for recording or exporting video/features;
- risk of re-identification from face/eye data;
- transparent handling of compensation and conflicts of interest;
- sampling bias, device/lighting bias and exclusions;
- reporting failed calibration, missing data and adverse experiences;
- a participant-accessible results and complaint path.

Ordinary use is not consent to data collection. The V0 application must not create a dataset automatically. If a future protocol records personal data, the repository must not contain those data and public issue reports must use synthetic material.

No metric may be interpreted as a diagnosis. No result may be called clinically validated unless the precise claim is supported by an appropriate completed study and review.
